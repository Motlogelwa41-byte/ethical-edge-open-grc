**Project:** Cognitive GRC Engine (CGE) — Safeguard Room (`/app/room_safeguard`)

**Submission Target:** May 30, 2026

---

## 1. Project Innovation Statement
Local civil society organizations, humanitarian bodies, and development initiatives within the SADC region face immense operational friction verifying institutional compliance with complex safeguarding policies. Manual auditing introduces severe administrative delays, exposing vulnerable populations to unmitigated institutional risks.

Ethical Edge GRC Consulting is deploying a dedicated, modular RegTech infrastructure inside the **Ethical Edge Open GRC** ecosystem. This specialized AI module automates safeguarding risk scoring, personnel background vetting protocols, and data sovereignty compliance checks, shrinking a standard 21-day administrative manual verification process down to less than 48 hours.

## 2. Technical Approach & Architecture
Built cleanly using **Python, FastAPI, and modular JSON schemas**, the system decouples broad governance requirements from specific protective actions. The `room_safeguard` module operates on a dedicated backend router that dynamically evaluates institutional data inputs against three core pillars:

1. **Child Protection & Vulnerability Scoring:** Automatically scanning organizational infrastructure profiles for operational warning signs (e.g., unsecured digital records of minors or inadequate reporting pathways) mapped against the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child.
2. **Operational Integrity & Anti-Exploitation:** Correlating supply chain data and vendor relationships with **King V Principle 1 (Ethical Leadership)** to catch conflicts of interest, coercive workplace environments, or financial integrity flags.
3. **Beneficiary Data Sovereignty:** Auditing local data storage parameters against the **Botswana Data Protection Act (DPA)** to ensure zero non-consent tracking of vulnerable beneficiaries.
