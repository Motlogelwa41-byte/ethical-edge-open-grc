# Internet Society (ISOC) Grant: SADC Internet Resilience & Trustworthy AI

## 1. The Applicant
* **Company:** Ethical Edge GRC Consulting (Pty) Ltd
* **Location:** Gaborone, Botswana (SADC Region)
* **Registration:** BW00009434846
* **Mission:** Strengthening regional digital governance through AI.

## 2. The Problem
Digital infrastructure in the SADC region is growing fast, but it is vulnerable. Many regional ISPs and regulators lack the tools to audit **Internet Resilience** and **Data Sovereignty**. Without oversight, AI deployments in Africa risk being unethical or insecure.

## 3. The Solution: ISOC Resilience Room
We are using our **Cognitive GRC Engine** to audit the "Trust Layer" of the internet.
* **Internet Resilience:** Automated auditing of network governance.
* **Data Sovereignty:** Using the Engine to ensure AI systems follow local data laws.
* **Ethics by Design:** Using our `AI_ETHICS.md` and `ai_auditor_prompt.txt` to monitor AI fairness and security.

## 4. Budget Breakdown ($500,000 USD)

| Category | Amount | Justification |

| **Regional Research** | $150,000 | Mapping internet resilience across 16 SADC member states. |

| **Sovereignty Frameworks** | $100,000 | Building localized AI Ethics models for the African context. |
| **Regulator Training** | $75,000 | Workshops for SADC officials on using the GRC Engine. |

## 5. Technical Logic
The project uses **Python/FastAPI** to create a "Trust API" for regulators. It connects the logic in `engine_logic.py` with the ethical standards in `AI_ETHICS.md` to provide a real-time "Resilience Score" for internet providers.
